# XOM research brief

**Thesis:** Exxon posted solid but declining FY2025 results (net income $28.84B vs $33.68B; EPS $6.70 vs $7.84) as lower crude realizations outweighed record Permian/Guyana growth. Shares rallied 61% to $178.28, above both moving averages, with low beta (0.382) and FY2024 debt/equity of 0.158 (FY2025 total debt not reported). At 22.9x trailing P/E with a rising 10-year yield (5.09%), the stock trades near its $177.59 analyst target, suggesting a hold.

## Bull case
- Record Permian and Guyana production (Prosperity FPSO) added $1.9B in earnings, with U.S. crude/NGL output up to 1,522 kbd from 1,248 kbd
- Low FY2024 leverage (D/E 0.158) and strong operating cash flow ($51.97B) underpin the ~2.3% dividend
- Low beta (0.382) limits sensitivity to broad market swings
- LNG growth target of 50M tons/year by 2030 signals a durable long-term growth lever
- Analyst consensus remains Buy with 22 analysts and a $177.59 mean target

## Bear case
- FY2025 net income fell 14.4% and EPS dropped from $7.84 to $6.70, driven by a $6.1B hit from lower crude price realizations amid rising industry supply
- Free cash flow dropped to $23.612B from $30.716B as capex rose to $28.358B, pressuring reinvestment flexibility
- Stock trades well above both 50-day and 200-day moving averages after a 61% one-year rally, and 26% annualized volatility implies meaningful price swings despite low beta
- Rising 10-year Treasury yield (5.09%) despite Fed easing could compress capex-heavy energy valuations
- Climate litigation (Colorado case before the Supreme Court), governance friction, and unresolved regulatory/energy-transition policy uncertainty add overhang

## Fundamentals
XOM trades at 22.9x trailing and 14.9x forward P/E (P/B 2.83x), TTM profit margin 9.07%, ROE 12.58%. FY2025 revenue, operating income, and net income all declined versus FY2024 (net income $28.844B vs $33.680B; EPS $6.70 vs $7.84). Operating cash flow fell to $51.97B and free cash flow to $23.612B as capex rose to $28.358B. Balance sheet leverage was conservative in FY2024 (D/E 0.158, total debt $41.71B vs $263.705B equity); FY2025 total debt was not reported, and cash fell to $10.681B from $23.029B.

## News and catalysts
Sentiment is neutral overall. Supportive factors include 29 ETFs net-buying $131.1M in XOM shares (Oct 14, including $83.8M from SPY), a stated LNG sales target of 50 million tons/year by 2030, a reported $4.4B margin tailwind ahead of earnings, and renewed oil-supply concerns from Middle East tensions lifting crude prices. Offsetting these are climate litigation risk from a pending Supreme Court case on Colorado climate-damages suits, ongoing regulatory/energy-transition policy uncertainty, public governance friction involving a shale magnate's board-seat dispute, and relative underperformance versus refiners like Marathon Petroleum and Valero in at least one session.

## Market and macro
XOM closed at $178.28, up 9.01% over the past month and 61.14% over the past year, trading above its 50-day ($164.19) and 200-day ($152.01) moving averages in a confirmed uptrend. Annualized volatility is 26.01%, implying continued meaningful price swings even though a low beta of 0.382 limits correlation with broad market moves. Macro backdrop shows CPI up 3.72% YoY, Fed funds rate eased to 3.75% from 4.22%, but the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.09% from 3.99%, unemployment fell to 4.2%, and real GDP grew 2.19% YoY. The yield curve divergence (short rates down, long rates up) may reflect inflation persistence concerns affecting both discount rates and energy demand expectations.

## Conclusion
XOM presents a balanced risk/reward: strong production growth and resilient cash generation support the bull case, but declining earnings, falling free cash flow, elevated valuation after a 61% rally, rising long-term rates, and unresolved regulatory/climate-litigation risk warrant caution. With the stock trading near the analyst mean target of $177.59, current pricing appears to reflect most known positives, making this a hold-oriented profile rather than a fresh high-conviction buy; this is analysis, not investment advice.
